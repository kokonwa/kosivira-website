DrivePass is a proposed Kosivira platform where a vehicle owner keeps essential records in one place, tracks expiries and generates a controlled scan code for an authorised verification experience.

Prototype scope
Owner records + limited sharing

The owner uploads documents. A scan view displays only the minimum chosen information. Full official verification would require approved source-data partnerships.
